A calm corner of the internet

Skylroom is a free soundscape studio for meditation, yoga, sleep and deep focus. Layer gentle rain, ocean waves, a crackling fire, wind through the trees, distant thunder, birdsong, a Tibetan singing bowl, wind chimes and soft white or brown noise into a blend that fits your moment — then let it play while you breathe, study, or drift off. Everything is generated live in your browser, so there are no files to download and no loops to notice.

Built entirely with the Web Audio API — no audio files were used.
